About Me

My name is Elijah Renault and I'm a student in my senior year in the IT: Software Development program at NBCC's Saint John campus. I'm extremely passionate about IT, specifically video game design and development.

In high school, I completed a co-op program with Brilliant Labs. It cultivated the foundation for my passion, giving me the opportunity to learn more about the video game industry and careers in the field. As I complete my post-secondary education, I am looking for further exposure to the IT workforce.

In my free time, I've been slowly conceptualizing my own video game idea, a retro PS2-era-style platformer currently under the working title "Artopia". My dream is to develop this game as close to my vision as possible.
